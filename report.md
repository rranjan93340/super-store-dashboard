Super Store Sales Dashboard
The Retail Store Sales Dashboard is an interactive, visually engaging Power BI dashboard designed to provide actionable insights for retail businesses. It breaks down sales data into key metrics, enabling stakeholders to analyze customer preferences, shipping efficiency, and product profitability at a glance.

Key Features
✅ Regional Performance Analysis: Interactive filters for Central, East, South, and West regions, allowing easy comparison of sales trends across different areas.

✅ Payment Mode Insights: A dynamic doughnut chart illustrating customer preferences for payment methods—Cards, Online, and Cash on Delivery (COD).

✅ Sales & Profit Trends: Line charts tracking monthly sales and profit, highlighting seasonal trends and growth opportunities.

✅ Category & Segment Breakdown: Pie and bar charts displaying sales distribution by Segment (Consumer, Corporate, Home Office) and Category (Office Supplies, Technology, Furniture).

✅ Shipping Performance Overview: A bar chart analyzing the efficiency and popularity of various shipping methods, including Standard Class and Same Day delivery.

✅ Profitability Heatmap: A state-wise geographical map visualizing profit and sales, providing clear regional performance insights.

✅ Sub-Category Deep Dive: Focused analysis of high-performing sub-categories like Phones, Chairs, and Binders to uncover growth opportunities.

Business Impact
🔹 Identifies sales peaks and slow periods across different months and regions.
🔹 Optimizes shipping strategies by analyzing delivery method efficiency.
🔹 Enhances decision-making regarding customer payment preferences.
🔹 Highlights profitable product categories while pinpointing underperforming areas for targeted interventions.

This dashboard equips retail businesses with data-driven insights to boost revenue, improve efficiency, and refine strategic decision-making.
